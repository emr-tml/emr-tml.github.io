Grab both files and place them in root of USB drive.

Download PS3HEN.BIN
Download HEN_UPD.pkg

Plug the USB drive in the slot closest to disc drive on your PS3.

Click on *HEN Enabler*

HEN should enable and install.

REMOVE THE USB DRIVE and reboot when done!

** Do not leave pkg file on USB with it plugged into PS3 after installation! ** It will be found by HEN and reinstalled everytime you enable HEN!
